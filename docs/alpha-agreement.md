# Alpha Founding Partner Agreement

This sets out how the Kinward alpha works between Catapult and the organisation named below. A formal contract on these terms will follow before go-live.

## What you get

- **Kinward free of charge** for your pilot communities until the success criteria are met, with **no setup fee**, now or later.
- **We do the setup:** we build your new website alongside your current one, load your calendars, menus, apartments and photos, import your family contacts, set up texting and train your staff.
- **Support throughout,** a monthly scorecard, a feedback call every two weeks and check-ins at day 30, 60 and 90.

## What we ask of you

- A named decision-maker, and a staff champion in each pilot community.
- Your content and family contacts, and help taking the baseline measurements (a staff time diary, short resident and family surveys, and a few weeks of attendance counts).
- **The switch:** once the agreed website checklist is met, you point your domain to Kinward within the agreed number of days. The 90-day alpha starts on that day. If the switch doesn't happen, the alpha ends.
- Honest feedback, and a case study and reference if the results are met.

## How it ends

- **Results met:** your subscription starts the following month at the founding price, **Community Life $519 or Website $349 per community per month**, locked for 24 months. Your other communities can join at the same price.
- **Results not met at day 90:** the alpha extends free in 30-day steps, up to 180 days after go-live. If they're still not met, you choose to continue at the founding price or leave owing nothing. If your own commitments above weren't met, the free extension is at our discretion.
- **Leaving:** you can stop at any time before your subscription starts, or decline it within 10 business days of the results, and owe nothing. The website we built stays with us; your content does not.

## Your data

You own your content and can export it, including family contacts, at any time. You're responsible for consent to message families and to publish photos; we provide the tools. We may use anonymised, combined activity data to improve Kinward, and nothing that identifies a resident, family member or staff member leaves your organisation.
